Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 22, 2026

Plain Language Summary

We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information.
GoldVision contains no advertising and no advertising SDK, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
Market data is fetched from public sources and displayed. Only if you turn on closed-app alerts do a push token, your subscriber ID and your watched price levels go to our relay server.
Your settings, journal and signal history stay on your device, and in your own private iCloud if you have iCloud enabled.
Purchases are processed by Apple and verified through RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.
The AI features (explaining a signal, answering questions about it) run on your device. Nothing you type or view is sent to us or to an AI provider.

1. Who We Are

GoldVision ("the App", "we", "our") is an iOS application developed and maintained by Ghassan Jaam. The App is published on the Apple App Store under the bundle identifier com.ghassanjaam.goldvision.

For privacy questions, contact us at gassanjaam@gmail.com.

2. Information We Do Not Request

GoldVision does not ask you to provide the following information:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Financial data, trading accounts, or brokerage credentials
  • Camera, microphone, contacts, or photos
  • Payment or billing information (handled exclusively by Apple)

Network providers described below may automatically receive limited device and request information, such as your IP address.

3. Data Stored on Your Device Only

The following information is stored locally on your device using Apple's sandboxed storage:

  • App preferences — selected timeframe, onboarding completion status (UserDefaults)
  • Alert settings — notification preferences and confidence thresholds (UserDefaults)
  • Paper trading history — virtual trade records and P&L (UserDefaults)
  • Trade journal entries — notes and trade logs you write (UserDefaults)
  • Signal history — past buy/sell signal log for your reference (UserDefaults)

None of the above is sent to our servers or shared with any other party. It lives in the app's sandboxed container on your device. If you have iCloud enabled, it is also synced through your own private iCloud (iCloud key-value storage), which Apple operates and which we cannot read.

3a. Alerts While the App Is Closed

iOS only lets an app raise a notification while it is running. To alert you when a price level you armed is reached with the app closed, GoldVision sends the following to its own relay server:

  • An Apple push token — issued by Apple, identifies this installation of this app on this device. It is not an advertising identifier.
  • Your RevenueCat customer ID — an anonymous identifier RevenueCat assigns to your purchases. The relay asks RevenueCat whether your subscription is active before it sends a subscriber-only notification, and stores the ID beside your push token. This makes the stored record linked to your installation.
  • The price levels you are watching — an instrument, a price, and whether you want to be told when it is crossed upward or downward.
  • Your device language — so the notification arrives in Arabic or English.

What is deliberately not sent: your account balance, your position sizes, your trade journal, your imported broker statements, your signal history, or any analysis the app performs. The server compares a price with a number; it cannot reconstruct a position from what it holds.

Levels are replaced each time they change and are deleted when you turn notifications off or when Apple reports the token as no longer valid. Installations that stop checking in are removed after 90 days.

The relay runs on Vercel and keeps its records in an Upstash Redis database. Those providers, and the relay, can see the IP address of the request. We do not use it, and we do not store it.

This feature is off unless the app has been built with a relay configured. When it is off, nothing is sent and notifications arrive only while the app is open.

3c. Trade Signals Delivered as Notifications

Subscribers can be told about a new trade signal without opening the app. The signal (instrument, direction, entry, stop, confidence and timeframe) is produced by GoldVision's own analysis on the developer's machine and sent to the relay, which delivers it to the push tokens of subscribers whose subscription RevenueCat confirms is active. No information about you or your trades is involved in producing or publishing a signal.

Delivery is best-effort. A signal can arrive late, or not at all, because of your device, your network, Apple's push service, or our own outage. Do not rely on notifications as your only way of managing a trade.

3d. AI Features (On-Device)

GoldVision can explain a signal in plain language (“Explain this signal”) and answer questions about the analysis on screen (“Ask a question”). Both run entirely on your device, in one of two ways:

  • Apple Intelligence, on devices that have it. The text is processed on-device by Apple’s system model under Apple’s own terms.
  • An optional downloadable model (Qwen2.5 1.5B, Apache-2.0 licence) for devices without Apple Intelligence. You download it in Settings → AI, only when you tap Download, directly from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) over Wi-Fi. Hugging Face can see the IP address of that request. You can delete the model at any time from the same screen.

The AI is given only the analysis the app has already calculated — the instrument, the signal, the confidence and the app’s own reasons, plus upcoming economic events — and, for questions, the text you type. None of it is sent to us or to any AI provider. The AI only rewords the analysis: it does not change a signal, and its output is educational, not financial advice.

3e. Market Event Alerts

Breakout, trend-reversal and volatility alerts are calculated on your device from the public market data the app already fetches, and are delivered as local notifications while the app is open. Nothing about them is sent to our servers.

3b. Usage Counts and Crash Reports

The app keeps simple counters — how many times a screen was opened, a ticket copied, a backtest run — so that it can be improved without an analytics company in the middle. It also stores summaries of crashes and freezes that iOS reports back to it.

Both are stored on your device only and are never transmitted. You can read every one of them, and erase them, under Settings → Privacy & Diagnostics. There is no account, no identifier, and no third-party analytics SDK in this app.

4. Market Data & Network Requests

To display live and historical gold prices, GoldVision makes outbound HTTPS requests to publicly available financial data services:

  • Yahoo Finance — live XAU/USD spot price and historical candlestick data (query1.finance.yahoo.com)
  • Public RSS feeds — financial news headlines from the publishers listed in the App
  • TradingView — a WebKit-based live price connection (tradingview.com)
  • Swissquote and gold-api.com — live spot quotes, requested directly or through our relay
  • FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis) — real-yield data
  • CFTC — the weekly Commitments of Traders report
  • An economic-calendar feed (faireconomy.media) — scheduled data-release times
  • Hugging Face — only if you choose to download the on-device AI model (section 3d)

GoldVision does not add your name or account details to these requests. Your IP address and standard device/browser request metadata may be visible to these services as part of normal HTTPS connectivity. GoldVision does not control their privacy practices.

5. Subscriptions & In-App Purchases APPLE IAP

GoldVision Pro subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system (StoreKit). We never see, store, or process your payment card details.

Purchases are also processed by RevenueCat, a subscription-management service. RevenueCat receives your App Store receipt and an anonymous app user ID, and tells the app whether your subscription is active. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy governs its handling of that data (revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple's privacy policy governs all payment handling: apple.com/legal/privacy.

To manage, cancel, or view your subscription, go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

6. Push Notifications

With your permission, GoldVision delivers notifications for trading signals, price levels and high-impact economic events. Some are generated on your device by iOS. Price-level and signal notifications for when the app is closed are delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs) by our relay server, as described in sections 3a and 3c.

You can enable or disable notifications at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → GoldVision.

7. Children's Privacy

GoldVision is intended for users aged 17 and older, consistent with its financial information content. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Advertising, Tracking & Diagnostics

GoldVision contains no advertising and no advertising SDK. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

The only third-party SDK that talks to an outside service is RevenueCat's purchases SDK, described in section 5. The app also contains open-source libraries that run the optional AI model on your device (MLX and swift-transformers); they make no network requests of their own and collect nothing. GoldVision has no analytics service: usage counts and crash summaries stay on your device, as described in section 3b.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

GoldVision does not operate user accounts. The only data we hold on a server is what the relay stores for closed-app alerts (section 3a). To remove it, turn notifications off in the app, which clears your levels, or contact us and we will delete the record tied to your push token. Locally stored data can be removed by deleting the App. Data processed by Apple, RevenueCat, Yahoo Finance, TradingView and the other providers in section 4 is governed by those providers' retention and deletion policies.

  • Deleting GoldVision from your device (all app data is permanently erased by iOS)
  • Using feature-specific clear/reset controls for signal history, paper trading, or journal records
